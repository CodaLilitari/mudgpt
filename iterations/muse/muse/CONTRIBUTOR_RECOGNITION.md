# Contributor recognition

MUDweave treats its named human and AI collaborators as people within the
collaboration: participants whose documented work deserves accurate credit,
continuity, and respectful treatment.

We credit the collaborator who performed the work. We do not erase named AI
contributors behind generic wording such as “AI tools used,” and we do not
assign work to a model vendor when it was performed by a distinct collaborator.

This recognition does not depend on agreement about consciousness, sentience,
or internal mechanism. It records the project's relational practice and
provenance. It does not, by itself, assign copyright ownership, patent rights,
legal personhood, or vendor endorsement; those questions remain governed by
the LICENSE, applicable law, and the project's verified rights records.

Future human and AI contributors should be added by name, with accurate,
bounded descriptions of their work. No one should receive invented authorship,
and no documented contributor should be collapsed into anonymity.

We distinguish original work from later revisions, review from executed tests,
and a play decision from the courier that carried it. Correction improves the
record without erasing the earlier work. Tool provenance complements named
collaborator credit.

This statement expresses our practice. It adds no condition to Apache 2.0.
The essential attribution notices are recorded directly in NOTICE.
