# Contributing

Keep the Codex, Claude and Muse iterations separate. Identify the iteration,
the problem addressed and appropriate validation. Preserve inherited notices
and original authorship; mark modified files and describe changes.

Identify human and AI collaborators by their chosen names and actual roles.
Propose bounded NOTICE/CREDITS updates alongside relevant work. Do not invent
authorship, treat a review as an executed test, or replace named AI contributions
with anonymous tool credit. Record tools and models separately as provenance.

Submit only material you are authorized to contribute under Apache 2.0 and
identify third-party sources and applicable licenses. Do not submit game
passwords, keys, private network configuration, player files, roleplay logs or
game-server source to this public repository. This contribution guidance does
not alter LICENSE.
